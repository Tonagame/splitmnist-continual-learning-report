Sequential learning creates forgetting

The model sees digit pairs one after another. If it only trains on the newest pair, old classes fade from the decision boundary.

Variants

LSR-lite + Fourier Adds a small spectral regularization guardrail.
LSR-lite + ASW Adapts KD and feature weights from old/new loss ratio.
